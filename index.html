👋 Hello, I am Alex

I'm a software engineer & data scientist fluent in Python, Go, SQL, Swift and C++, with over a decade of experience in building fast, reliable data pipelines.

I currently work at Hudson River Trading where I engineer high-performance systems to process massive volumes of trading and research data to fuel our machine learning AI models. I've built many analytics-heavy tools for the financial industry at Stripe and before that, I built & worked on core data infrastructure at Digital Ocean.

My work is rooted in cloud-native platforms like AWS and GCP, and powered by tools like Airflow, Glue, and BigQuery.

Outside of work, I blog & publish academic papers about topics concerning Random Matrix Theory, Machine Learning techniques for Compressed Sensing, and Information Theory amongst other things.

I received my Bachelor’s degree from the University of Pennsylvania, M.S. from the University of Texas at Austin, and my PhD in Computer Science from Yale University.
